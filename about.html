Our Story

Kind Crunchies was born out of a friendship between two people with very different habits but a shared love for chips. One couldn’t stop munching on packaged chips, and the other, always concerned about health, kept encouraging him to choose healthier options. After countless conversations about the struggle to find tasty yet nutritious snacks, they decided to create something that could bridge the gap.

And so, Kind Crunchies was founded with one simple mission: to help people enjoy their favorite crunchy snack, but in a way that’s healthier, better for you, and guilt-free. Every bag of our chips is a result of their passion for creating snacks that not only taste great but also nourish your body.

What Are Vacuum-Fried Chips?

At Kind Crunchies, we use a unique vacuum-frying process to create chips that are not only crispy but also retain more of the natural flavors and nutrients found in the vegetables. Unlike traditional frying, vacuum frying uses lower temperatures and less oil, which results in chips that are lower in fat and have a healthier profile. This method helps preserve the color, taste, and nutritional value of the vegetables, making every crunch a healthier choice.
